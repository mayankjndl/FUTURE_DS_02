# 📊 Social Media Campaign Performance Analysis

This project presents an end-to-end analysis of social media advertising campaign performance using **Power BI**. The objective is to evaluate campaign effectiveness, efficiency, and profitability through data-driven insights and interactive visualisation.

---

## 🔍 Project Overview

The analysis focuses on answering key business questions such as:
- How well did the social media campaign perform overall?
- Which campaigns generated the highest engagement and conversions?
- How efficiently was advertising spend utilised?
- Which campaigns delivered the best return on investment (ROI)?

The project follows a structured analytics workflow, from KPI creation and campaign comparison to actionable business recommendations.

---

## 📈 Key Metrics Analysed

The following marketing and performance metrics were analysed:

- Total Impressions  
- Total Clicks  
- Total Conversions  
- Click-Through Rate (CTR)  
- Cost Per Click (CPC)  
- Return on Investment (ROI)  

Custom **DAX measures** were created to ensure accurate calculation of derived metrics such as CTR, CPC, ROI, and Cost per Conversion.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

The Power BI dashboard includes:

- High-level KPI cards for quick performance assessment  
- Campaign-wise comparison of clicks, CTR, ROI, and conversions  
- Conversion and cost efficiency analysis  
- Cost per Conversion by campaign  
- Spend vs Clicks scatter analysis with bubble size representing conversions  
- Audience-level engagement insights  
- Interactive filtering for focused and dynamic analysis  

---

## 🧠 Key Insights

- High click volume does not always translate into higher profitability  
- Some campaigns achieved strong ROI despite operating at lower scale  
- Cost per conversion proved to be a more reliable optimisation metric than clicks alone  
- Campaign efficiency varied significantly, indicating scope for optimisation in targeting and creatives  

---

## ✅ Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

- Scale campaigns that consistently deliver high ROI  
- Optimise low-CTR campaigns by improving creatives or audience targeting  
- Prioritise cost per conversion and ROI over raw engagement metrics  
- Monitor spend efficiency closely while scaling high-volume campaigns  
- Maintain data quality by excluding unreliable or misleading dimensions
